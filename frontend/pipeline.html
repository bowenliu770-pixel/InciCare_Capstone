Processing Pipeline

4-Stage Engine

Every incoming alert flows through this 4-stage pipeline — from ingestion to routing — with AI classification at its core.

1
Multi-Source Ingestion ● Active

Real-time email monitoring via IMAP IDLE, webhook listeners, and syslog streams. New alerts are detected and ingested within milliseconds of arrival.

IMAP IDLE Webhooks Syslog
↓
2
AI Classification & Location Extraction ● Active

Each alert is analyzed by a local LLM (Llama 3.1 8B via Ollama) that reads the full email body. It classifies severity (Critical/Medium/Low) and simultaneously extracts real-world location names for map plotting.

Llama 3.1 8B Ollama 100% Local Location NER
↓
3
Severity Triage, SLA & Geocoding ● Active

Critical (Tier 1): 8 min SLA • Medium (Tier 2): 40 min SLA • Low (Tier 3): 4 hour SLA. Countdown timers start immediately. Extracted locations are geocoded via Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) and pinned on the incident map.

Tier 1: 8m Tier 2: 40m Tier 3: 4h Nominatim
↓
4
Multi-Channel Routing ● Active

Tier 1: SMS + Voice + Push • Tier 2: Team push notifications • Tier 3: Silent audit logging. Browser notifications, sound alerts, toast popups, and real-time map markers for all events.

SMS Voice Push Map Pins Audit Log
